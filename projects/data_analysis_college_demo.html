Context

This Sales Analysis project aimed to answer key questions by presenting findings in a dashboard: "Who are the customers?" and "How can the quantitative data of customers be related to their spending scores to better predict future customers' spending potential?"

Utilizing Age, Gender, and Annual Income as explanatory variables and Spending Score as the response variable, the project performed extensive data cleaning and data analysis. Data cleaning systematically reduced the initial 105 records to 88 by addressing duplicate rows, filling 29 missing values with the mean and the median based on context, identifying and handling 4 outliers in 'Annual Income', and correcting inconsistent data.

A significant finding from a dual-axis plot was a positive correlation between annual income and spending score for customers aged below 45, which reverses to a negative correlation for those aged 45 and above.

This project used Python packages including pandas, matplotlib, numpy, seaborn, math, statistics, and itertools.
